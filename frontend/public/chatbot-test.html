🎯 What Makes a Good Response

  • Understanding: Shows it "got" what you meant despite how you said it
  • Actionable: Includes at least one immediately implementable step
  • Specific: Not vague advice like "just focus harder"
  • Empathetic: Validates feelings and normalizes ADHD struggles
  • Personal: Acknowledges your streak, mood, or hours if mentioned
  • Encouraging: Ends with motivation or support
